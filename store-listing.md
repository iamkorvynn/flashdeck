## App name

## Short description (80 chars)
Flashcards with SM-2 spaced repetition. Learn faster, remember longer.

## Full description
FlashDeck is a simple, fast flashcard app built around SM-2 spaced
repetition — the algorithm behind SuperMemo and Anki.

- Create decks and add cards in seconds
- Study with tap-to-flip cards and grade your recall: Again, Hard, Good, Easy
- SM-2 schedules each card for you: reviews come back right before you'd
  forget them, so you spend time on what you don't know
- Due queue, 7-day forecast, streaks and retention stats keep you honest
- Search across all your cards, and back everything up with JSON export

Everything is stored on your device. No account, no ads, no internet
needed, no demo data — your decks are yours from the first card.

## Privacy
FlashDeck collects no data. All decks, cards and review history stay in a
local database on the device. The app makes no network requests.
